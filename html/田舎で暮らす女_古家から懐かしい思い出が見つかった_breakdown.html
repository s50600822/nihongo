07 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Dialect Copulas and Particles
わ, や, やし, やで, やねえ, やろ, やけど, ええ, ほんまに, あかん, わからん, かもしれん. The emotional and assertive texture of rural Kansai narration: soft assertion, tag questions, concessive connections, and negative forms.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃう (Completion / Regret Auxiliary)
起こしちゃった, 割れちゃった, 行っちゃった, 割れてきちゃう. "Unintentionally woke [her] up;" "regrettably cracked;" "went [missing];" "will end up cracking." The grammar of unintended consequences in animal care, weather damage, and searching.
〜ておく / 〜とく (Preparatory Auxiliary)
しとくわ. "[I'll] leave [it] [prepared]." The grammar of advance preparation and considerate inaction in beetle care.
Sufferer Passive (〜にやられる)
鳥にやられた, 店頭虫でやられて. "Damaged by birds;" "being affected by ladybugs." The grammar of suffering damage, essential for gardening and animal-keeping narration.
Directional Auxiliaries (ていく / てくる)
出てくる, 乾いてきてる, 枯れてくる, 割れてきちゃう, 広がてきた, 冷やしていく. The grammar of processes approaching visibility: beetles emerging, surfaces drying, plants withering, fruits cracking, vines spreading, beetles cooling.
Telegraphic Imperatives and Effort Grunts
どけ, 押すや, よいしょ, ほら, ちょっと待って. The grammar of urgent physical action: moving obstructions, pushing containers, exerting force, directing attention, and pausing for discovery.
ASR Uncertainty and Homophone Confusion
買う/飼う, 口て/朽ちて, 店頭虫/天道虫, 貸してる/被せてる, 申してる/言ってる. The grammar of machine-transcribed rural speech where homophones, dialect, and acoustic degradation create lexical ambiguity.
Nostalgia and Childhood Vocabulary
懐かしい, 生き物スケッチブック, カブトエビ, アメリカザリガニ, 仏の座, パリパリ, レース, 自転車の籠. The lexicon of Japanese childhood memory: nature observation books, prehistoric crustaceans, invasive crayfish, ornamental trees, brittle pressed flowers, treasured lace, and bicycle baskets.
Gardening and Beetle-Care Technical Terms
雨目, 割れる, ネット, カブトムシ, メス, オス, 冷やす, なってる. The specialized vocabulary of Japanese home gardening and beetle keeping: rain cracks, protective netting, sexual differentiation, temperature management, and growth monitoring.
