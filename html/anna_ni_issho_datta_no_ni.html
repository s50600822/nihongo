01 Verse 1

The evening sky metaphor suggests that the world itself has changed since they drifted apart. 違う色 (different color) is a poetic way of saying everything feels different now.

07 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

〜のに
"Although / even though / despite" — concessive particle expressing regret, disappointment, or emotional contrast. The song's emotional core.
〜て + いく / いる
Te-form + いく = action continuing into the future / away from speaker. Te-form + いる = progressive or state of being.
〜ばかり
"Only; merely; nothing but" — expresses limitation or repetitive action. Here: "does nothing but wander."
〜なんて
Dismissive quotative — "things like ~ / mere words like ~." Belittles what precedes it.
〜たい + ん + だ + ね
Desiderative + explanatory + tag particle — "It is that you want to ~, isn't it?" Empathetic observation.
〜くらいに / 〜ぐらいに
"To the extent that / so much that" — expresses degree or extent of an emotion or action.
〜ように
"So that / in order that" — expresses purpose or wish. Here: "so that those eyes may not waver."
〜始める
Auxiliary: "to begin to ~" — attached to verb stem. Here: 動き始めた (began to move).
