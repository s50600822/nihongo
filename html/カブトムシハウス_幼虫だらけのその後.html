05 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Copula and Sentence Endings: や / やで / んやつ / かい
ぷりぷりや (are plump = だ), 乱入やで (is an intrusion = だよ), どっから来たんやつ (where did [they] come from = のだろうか), 泣かんのかい (won't [you] cry? = か). The grammar of Kansai dialect sentence-final copula and modal particles in beetle and goat care contexts.
〜てみる (Attemptive) and 〜てくる / 〜ていく (Directional)
作ってみて (trying making), 買ってきた (bought and came), 行っていく (go [gradually]), 分けていきます (will go separate). The grammar of attempted actions and directional movement in beetle house construction and larva separation.
〜やすい / 〜にくい / 〜がち (Ease, Difficulty, and Tendency)
お世話がしやすい (easy to care for), 開けやすかった (was easy to open), 乾燥しやすい (prone to drying out). The grammar of ease/difficulty suffixes and tendency markers describing beetle house maintenance conditions.
〜っぱなし (Leaving in a State) and 〜ちゃう (Completion)
開けっぱなし (left wide open), 乾いちゃう (dries right away), 目覚めちゃった (woke up). The grammar of states left unattended and completed actions with connotation of accident or suddenness.
〜たらな / 〜んかなって思う (Speculative Reflection)
良かったんかなって思いました (I thought maybe it would have been good). The grammar of speculative self-reflection on past decisions using hypothetical past + んかな + quotative 思う.
〜てくれる / 〜てもらえる (Benefactive)
してもらえると嬉しいです (would be happy if [you] do [it] for me). The grammar of receiving favors and expressing gratitude for viewer support.
Onomatopoeia in Insect and Animal Contexts
ぷりぷり (plump/firm), カチッと (snap/click), ペロペロ (licking), おいしょ (heave-ho). The grammar of Japanese sound-symbolic vocabulary describing larva texture, enclosure mechanisms, animal behavior, and physical effort.
