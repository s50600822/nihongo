Kansai Copula and Sentence Particles: や / やん / わ / な / やろ

〜てしまう / 〜ちゃう (Completion and Regret)
持ってきちゃった (brought along), 隠れちゃった (hid), 見えちゃった (got seen), 行っちゃった (went and is gone). The grammar of completed actions with connotation of accident, suddenness, or irreversibility.
〜てくる / 〜ていく / 〜てきた (Directional)
出てきて欲しい (want [them] to come out), 集まってきた (gathered and came), 食べていきます (will go eat), 積んできた (picked and brought). The grammar of movement toward and away from the speaker in foraging and picnic contexts.
〜そう (Appearance) and 〜っぽい (Resemblance)
美味しそう (look tasty), 気持ち良さそう (look comfortable), 喜びそう (seem happy), 赤耳ガエルっぽい (looks like a red-eared frog), 黄色っぽい (yellowish). The grammar of perceptual appearance and resemblance in wildlife identification.
〜のに (Concessive) and 〜けど (Contrastive)
天気良かったのに (even though the weather was good), 木登りやったのに (even though [I] was tree-climbing), でかかったのに (even though [they] were big), ちっちゃいけどな (small, though). The grammar of concession and contrast in nature observation.
〜やないか / 〜んちゃうかな (Kansai Negative Questions)
ミシシッピアカミミガエルやないか (isn't this a red-eared slider), でかかったんちゃうかな (I wonder if they were big). The grammar of Kansai speculative and confirmatory questions.
