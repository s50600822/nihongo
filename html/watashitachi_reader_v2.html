About This Book
私たちが生きた20世紀
Watashitachi ga ikita nijisseiki

A collection of essays by 369 notable figures reflecting on the turbulent 20th century. This companion provides vocabulary, grammar, phrases, and tools to help you read the original Japanese text.

How to use

These are high-frequency words you will encounter across many essays. Use the search box to filter across all tables. Add your own words in the My Notes tab as you read.

How to use

These grammar patterns appear frequently in reflective, literary, and historical essays. Each box shows the pattern, romaji, meaning, and a typical context from 20th-century memoir writing.

〜を振り返る

Meaning: to look back on ~, to reflect on ~

Example: 戦後五十年を振り返る — "looking back on fifty years since the war"
