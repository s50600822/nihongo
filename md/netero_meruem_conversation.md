# Netero vs Meruem — Full Conversation Breakdown

**Source:** *Hunter x Hunter* manga chapters 297-298 / Anime episodes 122, 126  
**Theme:** The philosophical confrontation between humanity and the Chimera Ant King

---

## Full Conversation (Key Lines)

> **Meruem:**  
> 「なぜ戦う？  
> 其の方に勝ち目はない。  
> 死に急ぐことはあるまい。」
>
> **Netero:**  
> 「やってもみねえで分かるかよ。」
>
> **Meruem:**  
> 「……理解できぬな。  
> 人類という種のためか？  
> ならば余の行為はむしろ協力だと言っておく。  
> 例えばお前たちの社会には国境と言う縄張りに似た仕切りがあるだろう。  
> 右の境では子供が飢えて死に、左では何もしないクズが全てを持っている。  
> 狂気の沙汰だ。  
> 余が壊してやる。  
> そして与えよう。  
> 平等とは言えぬまでも、理不尽な差のない世界を。  
> 初めのうちは力と恐怖を利用することを否定しない。  
> だがあくまでそれは秩序維持のためと限定する。  
> 弱く……しかし生かすべきものを守るためだ。  
> 敗者を虐げるためでは決してない。  
> 貴様とは戦う。」
>
> **Netero:**  
> 「いけねえ相談だな。  
> 蟻の王メルエム……  
> お前さんは何もわかっちゃいねぇよ……  
> 人間の底すらない悪意を……！！  
> 俺は一人じゃねェ。  
> 人間を舐めるなよ、メルエム。  
> そう…貴様は……  
> 詰んでいたのだ。  
> 初めから。」

## Section 1: The Opening Exchange

### Line 0a — Meruem

**Japanese:** お主らに家畜以上の感情を用いなかったか。  
**Romaji:** Omoshi ra ni kachiku ijou no kanjou o mochi inakatta ka.  
**English:** "Did I not consider you as nothing more than livestock?" (lit. "Did I not hold feelings toward you as more than livestock?")

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| お主ら | omoshi ra | Pronoun | you (plural) — archaic, royal address |
| に | ni | Directional particle | toward; to |
| 家畜 | kachiku | Noun | livestock; domestic animal |
| 以上 | ijou | Particle | more than; above; beyond |
| の | no | Genitive particle | of |
| 感情 | kanjou | Noun | feelings; sentiment |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |
| 用い | mochi | Verb (continuative) | hold; use — continuative of 用いる *mochiiru* |
| なかった | nakatta | Auxiliary (negative past) | did not — past negative |
| か | ka | Question particle | question marker |

**Grammar Note:** お主ら is an archaic second-person plural pronoun fitting Meruem's royal speech. 家畜以上 means "more than livestock" — Meruem is reflecting on how he previously viewed humans as merely food/livestock. The rhetorical question format (〜なかったか) is a form of self-reflection.

### Line 0b — Meruem

**Japanese:** 今違う。  
**Romaji:** Ima chigau.  
**English:** "Now I think differently."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 今 | ima | Adverb | now |

**Grammar Note:** An extremely compressed statement. 違う is a plain-form verb meaning "to differ / be different." The subject (I / my thinking) is omitted. This two-word line marks a seismic shift in Meruem's worldview after meeting Komugi.

**Grammar Note:** 活かすに足る is an attributive phrase modifying 人間: "humans who are worthy of being kept alive." わずかながら is a concessive adverbial meaning "though only a few / if only slightly." This line is pivotal: Meruem acknowledges that his previous view of humans as mere livestock was incomplete.

### Line 0d — Meruem

**Japanese:** 今なら矛を収めるのであれば許してやらんでもない。  
**Romaji:** Ima nara hoko o osameru no de areba yurushite yaran de mo nai.  
**English:** "If you lay down your spear now, I may forgive you." (lit. "If you were to put away your spear now, it is not that I would not forgive you.")

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 今なら | ima nara | Adverbial phrase | if now; if it is now — conditional |
| 矛 | hoko | Noun | spear; halberd — metaphor for hostility/weapons |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |
| 収める | osameru | Verb (conditional) | put away; stow; surrender — conditional form |
| の | no | Nominalizer | nominalizes the clause |
| であれば | de areba | Conditional copula | if it is; if it were — formal conditional of だ |
| 許してやん | yurushite yaran | Verb phrase | would forgive — 許す + てやる (to do for someone) in negative volitional |
| で | de | Gerundive particle | even; also |
| も | mo | Inclusive particle | even; also |
| ない | nai | Adjective (negative) | not; nonexistent |

**Grammar Note:** 〜てやらんでもない is a double-negative construction meaning "it is not that I would not ~" i.e., "I might possibly ~." This is a very indirect, aristocratic way of making an offer. Meruem uses the metaphor of 矛 (spear) for Netero's combat stance. The construction softens the offer with royal haughtiness: "I suppose I might forgive you."

### Line 4 — Netero

**Japanese:** やってもみねえで分かるかよ。  
**Romaji:** Yatte mo minee de wakaru ka yo.  
**English:** "How would you know without trying?" (lit. "Without even doing and seeing, you understand?")

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| やって | yatte | Verb (te-form) | doing — te-form of やる *yaru* (to do) |
| も | mo | Inclusive particle | even; also |
| みねえ | minee | Verb (negative) | without seeing — rough negative of 見る *miru* |
| で | de | Gerundive particle | without ~ing |
| 分かる | wakaru | Verb | to understand; to know |
| か | ka | Question particle | question marker |
| よ | yo | Final particle | emphatic/assertive — "I tell you" |

**Grammar Note:** やってもみねえで is a rough, masculine contraction of やってもみないで (without even trying/doing and seeing). Netero shifts from the deferential わし speech to rough masculine slang when provoked. みねえ = みない (colloquial negative). The よ particle adds forceful assertion.

|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| それ | sore | Pronoun | that — refers to Meruem's offer |

| だろう | darou | Auxiliary | probably; I suppose — conjectural |

**Grammar Note:** Netero switches to わし, the elderly masculine first-person pronoun, signaling his true identity as an old man. だけの話 means "a matter only for ~ / applies only to ~." The conjectural だろう softens the refusal into a rhetorical question: Meruem's offer of forgiveness only applies to Netero individually, not to humanity as a whole.

## Section 3: The King's Declaration

### Line 11 — Meruem

**Japanese:** 余が壊してやる。  
**Romaji:** Yo ga kowashite yaru.  
**English:** "I will destroy it." (lit. "I will break it for you.")

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 余 | yo | Pronoun | I — royal first-person |
| が | ga | Subject particle | marks the subject |
| 壊してやる | kowashite yaru | Verb + auxiliary | will destroy — 壊す + てやる |

**Grammar Note:** 〜てやる usually means "to do something for someone" (from a superior position). Here it carries an ironic, contemptuous tone: "I will destroy it (for your own good)." The single line stands as a complete declaration of war against the existing world order.

---

### Line 12 — Meruem

**Japanese:** そして与えよう。  
**Romaji:** Soshite ataeyou.  
**English:** "And I shall grant." / "And I will bestow."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| そして | soshite | Conjunction | and; and then |
| 与えよう | ataeyou | Verb (volitional) | I shall give; I will bestow — volitional form of 与える *ataeru* |

**Grammar Note:** The volitional form (与えよう) expresses the speaker's will or determination. It is a formal, declarative mood fitting for a royal proclamation. The object is deliberately omitted, creating suspense before the next line.

---

### Line 13 — Meruem

**Japanese:** 平等とは言えぬまでも、理不尽な差のない世界を。  
**Romaji:** Byoudou to wa ienu made mo, rifujin na sa no nai sekai o.  
**English:** "A world without unjust disparity, if not equality." (lit. "Although one cannot call it equality, a world without unreasonable differences.")

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 平等 | byoudou | Noun | equality |
| とは | to wa | Quotative + topic | as for calling it ~ |
| 言えぬ | ienu | Verb (negative potential) | cannot say — negative potential of 言う *iu* |
| までも | made mo | Adverbial particle | even; to the extent that; although |
| 理不尽な | rifujin na | Na-adjective | unreasonable; unjust; irrational |
| 差 | sa | Noun | difference; disparity; gap |
| の | no | Genitive particle | of |
| ない | nai | Adjective (negative) | without; nonexistent |
| 世界 | sekai | Noun | world |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object (of the implied verb "grant") |

**Grammar Note:** 〜とは言えぬまでも is a concessive clause: "Although one cannot call it ~ / Even if one cannot say it is ~." The entire sentence is an object phrase with the verb omitted (carried over from 与えよう above): "[I shall grant] a world without unjust disparity." This is Meruem's most famous line, showing his nuanced philosophy: he rejects absolute equality as impossible but condemns unjust inequality as fixable.

| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |
| 否定しない | hitei shinai | Suru-verb (negative) | will not deny — negative of 否定する *hitei suru* |

**Grammar Note:** [Verb] + ことを否定しない = "will not deny [doing] ~." Meruem acknowledges that his revolution will require violence at the outset. The structure is a double nominalization: 利用すること (the act of using) is the object of 否定しない.

### Line 15 — Meruem

**Japanese:** だがあくまでそれは秩序維持のためと限定する。  
**Romaji:** Da ga aku made sore wa chitsujo iji no tame to gentei suru.  
**English:** "But I limit that solely to maintaining order."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| だが | da ga | Conjunction | but; however |
| あくまで | aku made | Adverb | solely; purely; to the end |
| それ | sore | Pronoun | that — refers to using power/terror |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for that |
| 秩序維持 | chitsujo iji | Noun | maintaining order; order preservation |
| の | no | Genitive particle | for; of |
| ため | tame | Noun | sake; purpose |
| と | to | Quotative particle | as; to the extent of |
| 限定する | gentei suru | Suru-verb | to limit; to restrict |

**Grammar Note:** あくまで is an emphatic adverb meaning "to the very end; purely; solely." 〜のためと限定する = "to limit [it] to the purpose of ~." Meruem draws a clear ethical boundary: violence is a temporary tool, not an end in itself.

### Line 16 — Meruem

**Japanese:** 弱く……しかし生かすべきものを守るためだ。  
**Romaji:** Yowaku…… shikashi ikasu beki mono o mamoru tame da.  
**English:** "To protect the weak…… yet those who deserve to live."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 弱く | yowaku | Adjective (adverbial) | weak — adverbial form of 弱い *yowai* |
| しかし | shikashi | Conjunction | yet; however; but |
| 生かす | ikasu | Verb (attributive) | to keep alive; to let live |
| べき | beki | Auxiliary | should; ought to; deserving of |
| もの | mono | Pronoun / Noun | those; ones (people) |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |
| 守る | mamoru | Verb | to protect; to defend |
| ため | tame | Noun | for the sake of; in order to |
| だ | da | Copula | is; it is |

**Grammar Note:** 生かすべきもの = "those who ought to be kept alive / those deserving of life." べき is an auxiliary expressing obligation or worthiness. The ellipsis after 弱く creates a dramatic pause, as Meruem struggles to articulate his newfound compassion without abandoning his royal authority.

### Line 17 — Meruem

**Japanese:** 敗者を虐げるためでは決してない。  
**Romaji:** Haisha o shitageru tame de wa kesshite nai.  
**English:** "It is never to oppress the defeated."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 敗者 | haisha | Noun | the defeated; losers; vanquished |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |
| 虐げる | shitageru | Verb | to oppress; to tyrannize; to abuse |
| ため | tame | Noun | for the purpose of; in order to |
| で | de | Copula (te-form) | is — te-form of だ |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for |
| 決して | kesshite | Adverb | never; by no means |
| ない | nai | Adjective (negative) | not; nonexistent |

**Grammar Note:** 〜ためではない = "it is not for the purpose of ~ing." 決して + negative = "never / by no means." This line is Meruem's explicit rejection of tyranny for its own sake, distinguishing his rule from mere domination.

### Line 18 — Meruem

**Japanese:** 貴様とは戦う。  
**Romaji:** Kisama to wa tatakau.  
**English:** "I will fight you."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|

| は | wa | Topic particle | as for — marks contrastive topic |

Its use by Meruem signals that despite his philosophical proposal, he considers Netero an enemy who must be defeated. The contrastive は (as for you [as opposed to others]) emphasizes that Netero is singled out for combat.

## Section 4: Netero's Rebuttal, Final Words & Detonation

### Line 19 — Netero

**Japanese:** いけねえ相談だな。  
**Romaji:** Ike nee soudan da na.  
**English:** "That's an unthinkable proposal." / "I can't accept that offer."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| いけねえ | ike nee | Adjective (negative) | no good; unthinkable — rough negative of いける *ikeru* |
| 相談 | soudan | Noun | consultation; proposal; discussion |
|  | da | Copula | is |
| な | na | Final particle | rough masculine sentence ender |

**Grammar Note:** いけねえ is a rough, masculine contraction of いけない (must not; no good). 相談 here refers to Meruem's offer/proposal. The tone is casual but firm refusal from an old man who has seen too much to be swayed by utopian promises.

### Line 20 — Netero

**Japanese:** 蟻の王メルエム……お前さんは何もわかっちゃいねぇよ……人間の底すらない悪意を……！！  
**Romaji:** Ari no ou Meruemu…… Omae-san wa nani mo wakaccha inee yo…… Ningen no soko saranai akui o……!!  
**English:** "Meruem, King of Ants…… You don't understand a thing…… humanity's bottomless malice……!!"

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 蟻 | ari | Noun | ant |
| の | no | Genitive particle | of |
| 王 | ou | Noun | king |
| メルエム | Meruemu | Proper noun | Meruem |
| お前さん | omae-san | Pronoun | you — rough but with mock-respectful -san |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for you |
| 何も | nani mo | Indefinite + particle | nothing; not anything |
| わかっちゃいねぇ | wakaccha inee | Verb (negative) | don't understand — rough contraction of わかっていない |
| よ | yo | Final particle | emphatic |
| 人間 | ningen | Noun | human; human being |
| の | no | Genitive particle | of |
| 底すらない | soko saranai | Adjective phrase | bottomless; without a bottom |
| 悪意 | akui | Noun | malice; ill will; evil intent |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |

**Grammar Note:** わかっちゃいねぇ is a heavily contracted form of わかっていない (don't understand). お前さん mixes the rough pronoun お前 with the honorific さん, creating Netero's characteristic mocking tone. 底すらない = "without even a bottom," i.e., "bottomless." This is Netero's most famous line, delivered just before activating the Poor Man's Rose. The manga famously places the furigana 進化 (shinka, evolution) over 悪意 (akui, malice), creating the chilling implication: humanity's malice is its evolution.

### Line 21 — Netero

**Japanese:** 俺は一人じゃねェ。人間を舐めるなよ、メルエム。  
**Romaji:** Ore wa hitori ja nee. Ningen o nameru na yo, Meruemu.  
**English:** "I'm not alone. Don't underestimate humanity, Meruem."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 俺 | ore | Pronoun | I — rough, masculine |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for me |

| じゃねェ | ja nee | Copula (negative) | am not — rough contraction of ではない |
| 人間 | ningen | Noun | humanity; humans |

| よ | yo | Final particle | emphatic |
| メルエム | Meruemu | Proper noun | Meruem |

**Grammar Note:** 舐める (nameru) literally means "to lick," but colloquially means "to underestimate / look down on." 舐めるな is the plain negative imperative. Netero reveals that his "self" in this battle is not individual but collective: he carries the entire human species' capacity for malice, embodied in the Poor Man's Rose bomb implanted in his body.

### Line 21c — Netero

**Japanese:** 地獄があるなら、またあおうぜ。  
**Romaji:** Jigoku ga aru nara, mata aou ze.  
**English:** "If there is a hell, let's meet again there."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 地獄 | jigoku | Noun | hell; underworld |
| が | ga | Subject particle | marks the subject |
| ある | aru | Verb (existential) | exists; there is |
| なら | nara | Conditional particle | if; if it is the case that |
| また | mata | Adverb | again |
| 会おう | aou | Verb (volitional) | let's meet — volitional form of 会う *au* |
| ぜ | ze | Final particle | rough masculine emphatic |

**Grammar Note:** One of the most chilling and iconic lines in the series. The volitional form 会おう expresses a proposal: "let's meet." Netero says this with a grin just before stopping his own heart to detonate the Poor Man's Rose. The casual, almost friendly tone (またあおうぜ = "let's hang out again") juxtaposed with the act of nuclear suicide creates an unforgettable moment. It implies that both Netero and Meruem are sinners destined for hell — Netero for the bomb, Meruem for his genocide.

### Line 22 — Netero

**Japanese:** そう……貴様は……詰んでいたのだ。初めから。  
**Romaji:** Sou…… Kisama wa…… Tsunde ita no da. Hajime kara.  
**English:** "Yes…… You…… had been checkmated. From the very beginning."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| そう | sou | Adverb | so; yes; that's right |
| 貴様 | kisama | Pronoun | you — insulting second-person |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for you |
| 詰んでいた | tsunde ita | Verb phrase | had been checkmated — 詰む + te-ita |
| の | no | Nominalizer | nominalizes the preceding clause |
| だ | da | Copula | is; it is that |
| 初め | hajime | Noun | beginning; start |
| から | kara | Ablative particle | from |

**Grammar Note:** 詰む (tsumu) is a shogi term meaning "to be checkmated." By using shogi terminology, Netero frames the entire battle as a game he had already won before it began. 詰んでいたのだ is an explanatory/emphatic construction (のだ) asserting a fact with conviction. The ellipses between words create a dying man's measured, final pronouncement.

## Section 6: Key Grammar Patterns Summary

| Pattern | Meaning | Example from Dialogue |
|---------|---------|----------------------|
| **〜てやる** | "To do something for someone" (from a superior position) — carries contemptuous or paternalistic tone | 壊してやる |
| **〜とは言えぬまでも** | "Although one cannot call it ~" — concessive clause expressing qualified agreement | 平等とは言えぬまでも |
| **〜ておく** | "To do something in advance / as preparation" — adds declarative or preparatory force | 言っておく |
| **〜ちゃいねぇ / 〜じゃねェ** | Rough masculine contractions of 〜ていない / ではない — Netero's signature rough speech | わかっちゃいねぇ、じゃねェ |
| **〜のだ / 〜んだ** | Explanatory/emphatic copula — asserts a fact with conviction or explanation | 詰んでいたのだ |
| **〜べき** | "Ought to; deserving of" — expresses obligation, worthiness, or moral desirability | 生かすべきもの |
| **〜まい** | Classical negative conjectural — "surely does not / surely will not" | あるまい |
| **〜ならば** | Formal conditional — "if that is the case / if so" | ならば |
