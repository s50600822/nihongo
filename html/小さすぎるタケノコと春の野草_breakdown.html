09 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Dialect Core — や, やけど, やから, やな, へん, おる, なか
板取りやけど (but), わらび好きやから (because), 適当やな (is, na), 言わへん (don't say), わからへん (don't know), 取らなか (didn't pick), おって (staying). The Kansai copula や, contrastive やけど, causal やから, negative へん, and existential おる form the emotional and grammatical backbone of rural Kansai narration.
〜ていく / 〜てくる (Directional Progressive)
調理していきます (will go cook), 火入れていきます (will go heat). The grammar of forward-moving processes in cooking and foraging narration.
〜ておく / 〜といく (Preparatory Action)
お皿入れときます (will put on plate in advance), 乗せといた (put on top in advance). The grammar of doing something in advance for future benefit.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Regret)
入っちゃった (got in), 摩りすぎちゃった (grated too much), 広まってしまう (unfortunately spread). The grammar of unfortunate completed actions.
〜そう (Appearance Inference)
美味しそう (looks delicious), ちっちゃくなりそう (seems like it will get tiny). The grammar of visual and sensory inference about food and plants.
Onomatopoeia and Sensory Texture
