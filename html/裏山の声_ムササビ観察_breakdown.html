Grammar: 動画じゃ分かりにくいけどすごいです。 uses the topic 動画じゃ (in video [topic]) + difficulty i-adjective 分かりにくい (hard to tell = 分かる + にくい) + contrastive けど (but) + i-adjective すごい (amazing) + polite です: "[It's] hard to tell in the video but [it's] amazing." The ASR error 星はます for すごいです is a severe phonetic degradation. もしかしてこの木這い回ってんの? uses the adverb もしかして (perhaps) + demonstrative この (this) + noun 木が (tree [subject], omitted) + progressive 這い回ってん (is crawling around = 這い回る + ている + ん contracted) + question の: "Could it be that this tree is crawling [with them]?" The ASR error 伸びくれてん for 這い回ってん is a severe phonetic mismatch. The vlogger is speculating that the tree is full of squirrels. この子がめっちゃ食べてるからってこと。 uses the demonstrative この子が (this child [subject]) + adverb めっちゃ (super) + progressive 食べてる (is eating) + causal から (because) + nominalizer ってこと (the thing is = と言うこと): "The thing is, this child is eating a lot." あれだけじゃないかも。 uses the demonstrative phrase あれだけ (only that) + negative speculation じゃないかも (maybe it's not): "Maybe it's not just that." えっと、今回3日間観察してみてったことはなんか案外近くにムササビがいてるんやなって思った。 uses the filler えっと (um) + topic 今回3日間観察してみて思ったことは (what [I] thought after trying to observe for 3 days this time [topic]) + adverb なんか (somehow) + adverb 案外 (surprisingly) + locative 近くに (close by) + subject ムササビが (musasabi [subject]) + progressive existential いてるん (are there = いる + ている + ん) + Kansai copula やな (is, na) + quotative って (that) + past 思った (thought): "Um, what [I] thought after observing for 3 days this time was, somehow [I] realized that musasabi are surprisingly close by." The Kansai んやなって思った is the quotative pattern for reporting one's realization. なんかいつも音とかが聞こえてるけど、そんななんか実際間近でムササビ見るってことなかったからなんか今回それはすごい良かったなって思いました。 uses the adverb なんか (somehow) + temporal いつも (always) + noun 音とか (sounds and such) + subject が ( [subject]) + progressive potential 聞こえてる (can be heard) + contrastive けど (but) + demonstrative そんな (such) + adverb なんか (somehow) + adverb 実際 (actually) + locative 間近で (up close) + object ムササビを (musasabi [object], omitted) + verb 見る (see) + nominalizer ってこと (the thing of) + negative past なかった (did not exist) + causal から (because) + adverb なんか (somehow) + topic 今回それは (this time that [topic]) + i-adjective すごい良かった (was really good = すごい + 良い + かった) + quotative なって (as) + polite past 思いました (thought): "Somehow [I] always hear sounds and such, but [I] had never actually seen a musasabi up close like that, so somehow this time [I] thought that was really great." The pattern [verb] + ってことなかった is the negative past experiential, meaning "had never [verb]ed." えっと、今回ムササビが飛ぶシーンはあんまり取れへんかったんけど、またこれはリベンジしたいと思います。 uses the filler + topic 今回ムササビが飛ぶーンは (the scene of the musasabi flying this time [topic]) + adverb あんまり (not very) + Kansai potential negative past 取れへんかった (couldn't capture = 取れる + へん + かった) + contrastive んけど (but = の + けど Kansai) + adverb また (again) + topic これは (this [topic]) + noun リベンジ (revenge; retry) + desiderative したい (want to do) + polite 思います (think): "Um, this time [I] couldn't capture the scene of the musasabi flying very well, but [I] want to try again [for] revenge." The loanword リベンジ is used in Japanese to mean a retry or rematch, not literal vengeance. それではまた次の動画でお会いしましょう。バイバイ。 uses the standard closing + loanword farewell. 06 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Dialect Core — おる, や, やん, やけど, へん, ちゃん, ねえへん, ほんま, うちら
おった (was there), 丸見えやこう (is completely visible), ちっちゃコロコロやん (tiny and round), 歌ってんやけど (is singing, but), 見えへん (can't see), 捕まえられへん (can't catch), ねえへん (won't go), ほんま待機中や (really on standby), うちら (we), 最高やな (is the best). The Kansai existential おる, copula や, negative へん, and pronoun うちら form the backbone of rural Kansai narration.
〜てん / 〜てる / 〜とる (Progressive Contractions)
音なってる (making sound), けがしてる (is injured), 入ってん (are inside), 落ちてん (are falling), 歌ってん (is singing), 起きてる (is awake), 返っとる (is flipped over), 這い回ってん (is crawling around), 食べてる (is eating), 聞こえてる (can be heard). The grammar of Kansai-contracted progressives where ている becomes てる, てん, or とる.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Disappearance)
起こしちゃった (woke [it] up unintentionally), 行っちゃった (went away), 間違えちゃう (might mistake), 行っちゃうか (will [it] go away?). The grammar of completed actions and unintended consequences.
〜そう (Appearance Inference) and 〜みたい (Resemblance)
遊んで欲しそう (as if wanting to play), 飛びそう (seems like [it'll] fly), 行きそう (seems like [it'll] go), ウサギうんこのバージョンみたい (like a version of rabbit poop), チョコボールみたい (like a Choco Ball). The grammar of visual inference and similative comparison.
〜たい (Desiderative) and 〜て欲しい (Request)
観察していきたい (want to go observe), カエル取りたい (want to catch a frog), ジョンどいて欲しい (want John to move), リベンジしたい (want to retry). The grammar of expressing desires and requesting others' actions.
〜てもいいですか (Polite Permission Request)
匂い嗅いでもいいですか (is it okay if [I] smell [it]?). The grammar of polite requests for permission using the te-form + もいいですか pattern.
Onomatopoeia and Sound-Symbolic Vocabulary
ぷにぷに (squishy), ふさふさ (fluffy), コロコロ / コロンコロン (round pellets), ほやほや (brand new), くしゃ (achoo). The grammar of Japanese sound-symbolic vocabulary describing texture, shape, freshness, and bodily functions.
Wildlife and Nature Vocabulary
ムササビ (Japanese giant flying squirrel), カエル (frog), 団子虫 (pill bug), カブトムシ (rhinoceros beetle), 裏山 (back mountain), 鳴き声 (animal call), 待機中 (on standby), 飛ぶ (fly/glide), 這い回る (crawl around). The specialized lexicon of Japanese rural wildlife observation.
ASR Errors and Phonetic Reconstruction
ささび→ムササビ, タぶタぶ→ぷにぷに, さび/さちゃん→ムササビ, チーなケなしてる→ちぃとけがしてる, レディケツのナ丸見えこう→これでケツの穴丸見えやこう, 熱勝する→汗かく, ひっくり帰っとる→ひっくり返っとる, あくした→あくしかった, 伸びくれてん→這い回ってん, 星はます→すごいです. The grammar of machine-transcribed rural Kansai speech where homophones, dialect phonology, and acoustic degradation create systematic lexical mismatches.
Imperatives, Prohibitives, and Urgency Markers
レッツゴー (let's go), 待つな (wait [command]), 行け (go!), 寄って (move closer), やめて (stop [it]), どいて (move). The grammar of commands and urgent requests in animal interaction and self-direction.
Quotative and Reported Realization Patterns
思った (I thought), んやなって思った (realized that), 良かったなって思いました (thought it was good), リベンジしたいと思います (want to retry). The grammar of reporting one's own thoughts and realizations in reflective narration.
Standard Vlog Outro Rhetoric
ご視聴ありがとうございました, チャンネル登録・高評価・コメントしてもらえると嬉しいです, InstagramとXのフォローもよろしくお願いします, メンバーシップもたまに更新しています, それではまた次回の動画でお会いしましょう. The formal grammar of Japanese YouTube audience engagement, contrasting with the casual body of the video.
