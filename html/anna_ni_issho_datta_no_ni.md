## Section 1: Verse 1

### Line 1

**Japanese:** あんなに一緒だったのに 夕暮れはもう違う色  
**Romaji:** Anna ni issho datta no ni, yuugure wa mou chigau iro.  
**English:** "Even though we were so close together, the evening sky is already a different color."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| あんなに | anna ni | Adverb | so; that much; to such an extent |
| 一緒 | issho | Noun / Adverbial noun | together; as one |
| だった | datta | Copula (past) | were — past tense of だ |
| のに | no ni | Concessive particle | although; even though; despite |
| 夕暮れ | yuugure | Noun | evening; dusk; sunset |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for |
| もう | mou | Adverb | already; now |
| 違う | chigau | Verb (attributive) | different — dictionary form modifying 色 |
| 色 | iro | Noun | color; hue |

**Grammar Note:** の is a concessive particle expressing regret or disappointment: "even though ~ / despite ~." It carries a sense of "and yet" with emotional weight. The evening sky metaphor suggests that the world itself has changed since they drifted apart. 違う色 (different color) is a poetic way of saying everything feels different now.

### Line 2

**Japanese:** ありふれた優しさは君を遠ざけるだけ  
**Romaji:** Arifureta yasashisa wa kimi wo toozakeru dake.  
**English:** "This ordinary kindness only pushes you further away."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| ありふれた | arifureta | Verb (attributive / past) | common; ordinary; ubiquitous — past of ありふれる *arifureru* |
| 優しさ | yasashisa | Noun | kindness; gentleness — nominalized from 優しい *yasashii* |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for |
| 君 | kimi | Pronoun | you — intimate second-person |
| を | o | Object particle | marks the object |
| 遠ざける | toozakeru | Verb | to distance; to keep at bay; to push away |
| だけ | dake | Limiting particle | only; merely; nothing but |

**Grammar Note:** 遠ざける is a transitive verb meaning "to distance / keep away." 〜だけ at the end of a sentence carries a sense of limitation or resignation: "it only does ~ / all it does is ~." The line suggests that generic, unthinking kindness has the opposite of its intended effect, driving the beloved away rather than bringing them closer.

**Romaji:** Tsumetaku kirisuteta kokoro wa samayou bakari.  
**English:** "This heart, coldly cast aside, does nothing but wander."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|

**Grammar Note:** [Verb] + ばかり means "does nothing but ~ / only ~s." It can carry either a sense of limitation or of continuous, repetitive action. Here it conveys both: the heart has been cast aside and now wanders aimlessly with no purpose. 切り捨てる is a strong verb combining 切る (to cut) + 捨てる (to throw away), implying a decisive, perhaps cruel rejection.

### Line 7

**Japanese:** 言葉ひとつ通らない 加速していく背中に今は  
**Romaji:** Kotoba hitotsu tooranai, kasoku shiteiku senaka ni ima wa.

| 言葉 | kotoba | Noun | word(s); language |

**Grammar Note:** [Te-form] + いく expresses an action that continues into the future or away from the speaker: "accelerating away." 背中に (toward the back) is a poignant image — the speaker can only watch the other person walk away. The sentence is left grammatically incomplete (に今は "now, toward..."), trailing off into silence, mirroring the failure of words to connect.

## Section 3: Verse 2

### Line 10

**Japanese:** 運命とうまく付き合って行くならきっと  
**Romaji:** Unmei to umaku tsukiatte iku nara kitto.  
**English:** "If one is to get along well with fate, then surely..."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 命 | unmei | Noun | fate; destiny |
| と | to | Comitative particle | with |
| うまく | umaku | Adverb | well; skillfully; smoothly — adverbial form of 上手い *umai* |
| 付き合って | tsukiatte | Verb (te-form) | associating; getting along — te-form of 付き合う *tsukiau* |

| きっと | kitto | Adverb | surely; certainly; no doubt |

### Line 11

**Japanese:** 悲しいとか寂しいなんて言ってられない  
**Romaji:** Kanashii to ka sabishii nante itterarenai.  
**English:** "One cannot afford to say things like 'I'm sad' or 'I'm lonely.'"

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 悲しい | kanashii | I-adjective | sad; sorrowful |
| とか | to ka | Listing particle | ~ or ~; things like ~ |
| 寂しい | sabishii | I-adjective | lonely; lonesome |
| なんて | nante | Quotative / dismissive particle | things like ~; such words as ~ (dismissive) |
| 言ってられない | itterarenai | Verb (negative potential) | cannot say; cannot afford to say — negative potential of 言う *iu* |

**Grammar Note:** 〜なんて is a dismissive quotative particle that belittles what precedes it: "mere words like ~ / things as trivial as ~." [Verb] + られない is the negative potential form: "cannot [verb]." Combined, the line means "I cannot (allow myself to) utter such weak words as 'sad' or 'lonely.'" It reflects a stoic determination to endure fate without complaint.

**Romaji:** Taikutsu na yoru wo tsubushitai n da ne.  
**English:** "You want to crush these tedious nights, don't you?"

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|

### Line 15

**Japanese:** ふぞろいな二人に今 たどりつける場所など無いんだ  
**Romaji:** Fuzoroi na futari ni ima, tadoritsukeru basho nado nai n da.  
**English:** "For us two, now mismatched, there is no place we can arrive at together."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| ふぞろいな | fuzoroi na | Na-adjective (attributive) | mismatched; uneven; ill-assorted |
| 二人 | futari | Noun | two people; the two of us |
| に | ni | Dative particle | for; to — marks the experiencer |
| 今 | ima | Temporal noun | now |
| たどりつける | tadoritsukeru | Verb (potential) | can reach; can arrive at — potential of たどり着く *tadoritsuku* |

| 無い | nai | Adjective (negative) | nonexistent; there is no |
| ん | n | Nominalizer | explanatory の |
| だ | da | Copula | is; it is that |

**Grammar Note:** ふぞろい literally means "uneven / mismatched" and originally refers to things that don't line up properly, like mismatched socks or an incomplete set. Here it describes two people who have grown apart and no longer fit together. たどり着く means "to reach after a struggle / to arrive at with effort" — the potential form たどりつける means "can reach." The explanatory のだ adds emphasis and a sense of resigned realization: "It is that there is no place."

### Line 17

**Japanese:** 不思議なくらいに魅せられてる 戸惑うくらいに  
**Romaji:** Fushigi na kurai ni miserareteru, tomadou kurai ni.  
**English:** "I am drawn to it, so much that it's mysterious — so much that I am bewildered."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 不思議な | fushigi na | Na-adjective (attributive) | mysterious; strange; wondrous |
| くらい | kurai | Adverbial particle | to the extent that; so much that |
| に | ni | Adverbial particle | to the point of; into |
| 魅せられてる | miserareteru | Verb (passive + progressive) | am being enchanted/drawn — passive progressive of 魅せる *miseru* |

The line repeats the structure with two different emotions: fascination (魅せられてる) and bewilderment (戸惑う). Despite the person feeling like a stranger, the attraction remains — which is precisely what makes it so confusing.

## Section 5: Bridge

### Line 18

**Japanese:** 心はどこにいる? どこに吹かれている? その瞳が迷わぬように  
**Romaji:** Kokoro wa doko ni iru? Doko ni fukarete iru? Sono hitomi ga mayowanu you ni.  
**English:** "Where is my heart? Where is it being blown? So that those eyes may not waver."

| Japanese | Romaji | Part of Speech | Gloss |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 心 | kokoro | Noun | heart; soul; mind |
| は | wa | Topic particle | as for |
| どこ | doko | Interrogative pronoun | where |
| に | ni | Locative particle | in; at |
| いる | iru | Verb (existential) | is; exists — for animate subjects |
| どこ | doko | Interrogative pronoun | where |
| に | ni | Locative particle | by; at — marks the agent/location of passive |
| 吹かれて | fukarete | Verb (passive te-form) | being blown — passive te-form of 吹く *fuku* |
| いる | iru | Auxiliary verb | is ~ing — progressive auxiliary |
| その | sono | Determiner | that; those |
| 瞳 | hitomi | Noun | eyes; pupils |
| が | ga | Subject particle | marks the subject |
| 迷わぬ | mayowanu | Verb (negative) | not waver; not stray — classical negative of 迷う *mayou* |
| ように | you ni | Adverbial particle | so that; in order that; like |

**Grammar Note:** The bridge uses a sequence of poetic, fragmented questions. 吹かれている is the passive progressive of 吹く (to blow, as wind): "is being blown [by the wind]." It evokes the image of a heart as a leaf blown aimlessly. 迷わぬ is the classical/literary negative of 迷う. 〜ように at the end of a clause expresses a wish or purpose: "so that ~ / in order that ~." The speaker asks where their own heart has gone, while wishing that the other's eyes never lose their way — a selfless prayer amid personal confusion.

| 動き | ugoki | Noun / Verb stem | movement; motion — stem of 動く *ugoku* |

| 君 | kimi | Pronoun | your — intimate second-person genitive |
| の | no | Genitive particle | 's |
| 情熱 | jounetsu | Noun | passion; fervor; ardor |

**Grammar Note:** [Verb stem] + 始める is an auxiliary construction meaning "to begin to [verb]." 動き始めた modifies 情熱: "the passion that has begun to move." Unlike the first chorus where the other person's back was accelerating away, here their passion is what moves — but words still cannot reach it. The speaker recognizes that the other person has found a new driving force, and it no longer includes them.

## Section 7: Key Grammar Patterns Summary

| Pattern | Meaning | Example from Lyrics |
|---------|---------|---------------------|
| **〜のに** | "Although / even though / despite" — concessive particle expressing regret, disappointment, or emotional contrast. The song's emotional core. | あんなに一緒だったのに |
| **〜て + いく / いる** | Te-form + いく = action continuing into the future / away from speaker. Te-form + いる = progressive or state of being. | 加速していく、吹かれている |
| **〜ばかり** | "Only; merely; nothing but" — expresses limitation or repetitive action. | 彷徨うばかり |
| **〜なんて** | Dismissive quotative — "things like ~ / mere words like ~." Belittles what precedes it. | 悲しいとか寂しいなんて |
| **〜たい + ん + だ + ね** | Desiderative + explanatory + tag particle — "It is that you want to ~, isn't it?" Empathetic observation. | 潰したいんだね |
| **〜くらいに / 〜ぐらいに** | "To the extent that / so much that" — expresses degree or extent of an emotion or action. | 不思議なくらいに |
| **〜ように** | "So that / in order that" — expresses purpose or wish. | その瞳が迷わぬように |
| **〜始める** | Auxiliary: "to begin to ~" — attached to verb stem. | 動き始めた |
