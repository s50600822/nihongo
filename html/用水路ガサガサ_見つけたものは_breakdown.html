08 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Dialect Core Particles and Copulas

The full spectrum of Kansai grammatical particles: assertive わ, copula や, tag やん, conjectural やろう, past やった, negative question やないか, obligation やんと, negative へん, explanatory んねんな, existential あんねん, animate existential おった/おられへん, conjectural かもしれへん, wonder なんやろう, resignation しゃあな, variant ちゃう, and intensifier めっちゃ.
〜てくる / 〜ていく (Directional Auxiliaries)
出てきた, 持って帰って, 集めていこう, 頑張っていきます, 上がってきた. "Came out;" "take home;" "will go collect;" "will go do [my] best;" "came up." The grammar of movement toward and away from the speaker, essential for describing exploration and return.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Regret)
落としちゃった, 入れちゃった, くらされちゃう. "Dropped [regrettably];" "put [them] all in;" "will get scratched [inevitably]." The grammar of accidental or regrettable completed actions.
〜たら (Conditional)
全部埋まったら畑に持ってく, 炊いたらめっちゃ美味しい, 雨降ったら水流れるや. "If [it] gets full, [I'll] bring [it] to the field;" "if [I] cook these, [they'll be] super delicious;" "if rain falls, water [will] flow." The grammar of future conditions and consequences in rural work.
〜のに (Concessive)
みんな水見たいのに, さっきまでおったのに取れた. "Even though everyone wants to see the water;" "even though [it] was there until just now, [I] got [it]." The grammar of frustrated contrast between desire and reality.
〜っぽい / 〜みたい (Similative)
ピンクっぽい, いそうじゃない. "Pinkish;" "doesn't [it] seem like [something is] there?" The grammar of approximation and visual inference in nature observation.
