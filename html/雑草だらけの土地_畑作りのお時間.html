Grammar: じゃあこっち。じゃあ。 uses the set phrase じゃあ (well then) + demonstrative こっち (this way), repeated for self-direction. 今日は前回雑草を刈った場所を耕して畑にします。 uses the topic 今日は (today [topic]) + temporal modifier 前回雑草を刈った場所を (the place where [I] cut weeds last time [object]) + verb 耕して (till = 耕す + て) + locative 畑に (into a field) + verb します (make/do): "Today [I'll] till the place where [I] cut weeds last time and make [it] into a field." The consecutive te-form 耕して connects the tilling action to the resultant state of becoming a field. The ASR error 買っ for 刈った is the persistent homophone confusion. The ASR error 2け for 畑 is a phonetic fragmentation. 今回は作業している合間によく質問にお答えします。 uses the topic 今回は (this time [topic]) + locative 作業している合間に (during the intervals of working = 作業 + している + 合間 + に) + adverb よく (often) + object 質問に (to questions) + honorific verb お答えします (will answer): "This time, between work, [I'll] answer questions [I] often receive." The pattern [verb] + 合間に means "during the intervals of [verb]." 最近熊被害についてのコメントをよくいただきます。 uses the temporal 最近 (lately) + topic 熊被害についての (about bear damage) + object コメントを (comments [object]) + adverb よく (often) + humble polite verb いただきます (receive): "Lately [I've] been getting many comments about bear damage." The humble いただく frames comment receipt as a favor from viewers. 私が住んでる地域では熊の出没目撃がかなり珍しく熊による被害はほぼありません。 uses the relative clause 私が住んでる (where I live = 住む + ている) modifying 地域では (in the area [topic]) + subject 熊の出没目撃が (bear appearance-sightings [subject]) + adverbial i-adjective かなり珍しく (quite rare = 珍しい + く) + topic 熊による被害は (damage caused by bears [topic]) + adverb ほぼ (almost) + negative existential ありません (does not exist): "In the area where I live, bear appearances and sightings are quite rare, and damage from bears is almost nonexistent." The pattern [noun] + による means "caused by [noun]." イノシシによる農作物への被害の方が多いです。 uses the comparative pattern [noun] + の方が多い (the [noun] side is more numerous = more common): "Damage to crops from wild boars is more common." The 方が construction marks a comparative preference or greater quantity. 皆さん電気柵で対応しています。 uses the subject 皆さん (everyone [subject]) + instrumental 電気柵で (with electric fences) + progressive polite 対応しています (are dealing with): "Everyone is dealing with [it] using electric fences." The ASR error 電気作 for 電気柵 is a phonetic near-miss where さく (saku) was misheard as さく (saku) homophone with 作. 我が家ではジョンがいるおかげなのかまだ被害に合っていません。 uses the locative topic 我が家では (at our house [topic]) + causal phrase ジョンがいるおかげなのか (perhaps thanks to John being here = おかげ + なのか) + adverb まだ (still/yet) + passive progressive 被害に合っていません (have not suffered damage = 被害に合う + ている negative): "At our house, perhaps thanks to John being here, [we] haven't suffered damage yet." The pattern [noun/verb] + おかげ(なのか) attributes a positive outcome to the preceding factor. どんな気配があるからなのでしょうか? uses the interrogative phrase どんな気配がある (what kind of sign exists) + causal から (because) + explanatory なのでしょうか (I wonder if it is that): "I wonder what kind of sign [they] sense [that keeps them away]?" The explanatory なのでしょうか marks speculative reasoning about animal behavior.

The sleeping creature is likely a frog or other water-dwelling animal disturbed during tilling, though the ASR omits the creature name. The completion 起こしちゃった marks the disturbance as an unintended, slightly regrettable consequence. クワを振るのは意と疲れます。 uses the nominalized clause クワを振るのは (swinging a hoe [topic] = クワを振る + の + は) + adverb 意外と (surprisingly) + adjective 疲れます (is tiring): "Swinging a hoe is surprisingly tiring." The nominalization of the verb phrase with の allows it to function as a sentence subject. 疲れた。 uses the past i-adjective alone for self-directed exclamation: "[I'm] tired." まだ1しかしてないけど大きな杭が出てきました。 uses the adverb まだ (still/yet) + number 1 + particle しか (only) + negative してない (haven't done = する + ていない) + contrastive けど (but) + subject 大きな杭が (a big stake [subject]) + directional perfective 出てきました (came out): "[I've] only done 1 [row/section] but a big stake came out." The ASR error 食い for 杭 is a phonetic mismatch where くい was interpreted as the verb 食う. The pattern [number] + しか + [negative verb] means "have only done [number]." どんどん耕していきます。 uses the adverb どんどん (steadily) + directional progressive していきます (will go till): "[I'll] keep tilling steadily." 出てきた。食べれそうなじゃがいも出てきた。 uses the directional perfective 出てきた (came out) repeated + potential appearance attributive 食べれそうな (seemingly edible = 食べる + れる + そう + な) modifying じゃがいも (potatoes): "[It] came out. Kansai Dialect Core — や, やだ, ちゃう, おらん/ある
晩御飯や (is dinner), や (exclamation), ちゃうかな (I wonder if it's wrong), めっちゃあるね (there's super much), ギリセーフや (barely safe), 降ったろうか (I wonder if it rained). The Kansai copula や, negative ちゃう, and explanatory/uncertainty patterns form the emotional texture of rural Kansai narration.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Regret)
起こしちゃった (woke [it] up [unintentionally]). The grammar of completed actions with unintended consequences, applied here to disturbing a creature while tilling.
〜たら (Conditional) and 〜だけ (Only)
ここまで来たら後は植えるだけです (if [I've] come this far, all that's left is planting), 育たなかったらジョンのご飯になります (if [they] don't grow, [they'll] become John's food). The grammar of conditional endpoints and limiting expressions in agricultural planning.
〜そう (Appearance Inference) and 〜みたい (Seeming)
遊んで欲しそうに (as if wanting to play), 食べれそう (seems edible), まだ食べれそう (still seems edible). The grammar of visual and contextual inference about edibility and desire.
〜てくれる (Benefactive) and 〜てもらう
うまく育ってくれるか (will [they] grow well for me?). The grammar of framing plant growth as a favor done for the gardener.
Reduplication and Mimetic Vocabulary
混ぜ混ぜ (mix-mix), ポツポツ (lightly raining), どんどん (steadily). The grammar of Japanese reduplicated verbs and onomatopoeia describing repetitive actions and gradual processes.
