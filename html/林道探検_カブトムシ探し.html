Grammar: めっちゃ大きい。でかない。 uses the Kansai adverb めっちゃ (super) + i-adjective 大きい (big) + Kansai tag question でかない (dekai + nai, [isn't it] huge?): "Super big. [Isn't it] huge?" The Kansai tag question attaches ない to the i-adjective stem to form a negative question seeking agreement, equivalent to standard でかくない? あれ何のライト? uses the demonstrative あ (that) + interrogative phrase 何のライト (what kind of light): "What light is that?" 2つ光ってるよ。 uses the number 2つ (two) + progressive 光ってる (are shining = 光る + ている contracted) + assertive よ: "[There are] two lights shining, yo." 3匹とも色違う。 uses the number+counter 3匹 (three [animals]) + universal particle とも (all/even) + noun 色 (color) + verb 違う (differ): "All three [are] different colors." The particle とも after a number + counter means "all [of them]." 真っ暗。すごいな。 uses the noun 真っ暗 (pitch black) + i-adjective すごい (amazing) + particle な: "Pitch black. Amazing, na." めっちゃ荒れてる。 uses the Kansai adverb + progressive 荒れてる (is overgrown/wild = 荒れる + ている contracted): "Super overgrown." これ多分イノシシ荒らしたとこや。 uses the demonstrative これ (this) + adverb 多分 (probably) + subject イノシシ (wild boar) + past attributive 荒らした (messed up) modifying とこ (place = ところ) + Kansai copula や: "This [is] probably a place wild boars messed up, ya." The Kansai や replaces standard だ. 横もボコボコ。 uses the noun 横 (sides) + particle も (also) + onomatopoeia ボコボコ (bumpy; pitted): "The sides [are] all bumpy too." The onomatopoeia ボコボコ describes the pitted, dug-up ground caused by wild boar foraging.

Grammar: 何もいてまへん。何もいてません。 uses the indefinite pronoun 何も (nothing) + Kansai progressive negative polite いてまへん (are not [here] = いる + て + まへん) + standard progressive negative polite いてません: "[There's] nothing here, mahen. [There's] nothing here, masen." The vlogger uses both Kansai まへん and standard ません, possibly for emphasis or addressing different audiences. The Kansai まへん is the polite negative form replacing ません. 明日かもやでっか。 uses the temporal 明日 (tomorrow) + conjectural かも (might) + Kansai copula や + i-adjective でっかい (huge): "[It] might be tomorrow, ya dekkai." The sentence is somewhat telegraphic, possibly meaning "[They] might [appear] tomorrow, [it's] huge [if they do]." けどこっちの家の樹液におるやつの方がめちゃくちゃでかい。 uses the concessive けど (but) + locative phrase こっちの家の樹液に (at the tree sap at this house over here) + Kansai progressive おる (is [there]) + nominalizer やつ (ones) + comparative particle の方が (the ones [are more]) + adverb めちゃくちゃ (way; incredibly) + colloquial i-adjective でかい (big): "But the ones [that are] at the tree sap at this house over here [are] way bigger." The comparative pattern [noun] + の方が + [adjective] is the standard Japanese way to express "[X] is more [adjective] than [Y]." The Kansai おる replaces standard いる for animate existence. 君はまだ小さい。 uses the pronoun 君 (kimi, you) + topic は + adverb まだ (still) + i-adjective 小さい (small): "You [are] still small." The pronoun 君 is commonly used when speaking to animals, children, or close friends in Japanese. もうちょっと頑張りな。 uses the adverbial phrase もうちょっと (a bit more) + imperative 頑張りな (ganbarina, try harder = 頑張る + な casual imperative): "[Try] a bit harder." The casual imperative 頑張りな is the Kansai/casual form, dropping the さい of 頑張りなさい. 飛んじゃった。 uses the past completion 飛んじゃった (flew away = 飛ぶ + てしまう past): "[It] flew away." The completion auxiliary ちゃった marks the flying as an accidental or slightly regrettable event. 大きいなあ。 uses the i-adjective 大きい + particle なあ (naa, exclamatory): "Big, naa." The elongated なあ expresses admiration. ここにもいてる。 uses the locative ここに (here) + particle も (also) + progressive いてる (are [here] = いる + ている contracted): "[There are some] here too."

Grammar: うわ、うわ、カブトムシでかい。 uses the interjection うわ (wow; whoa) repeated + noun カブトムシ (rhinoceros beetle) + colloquial i-adjective でかい (huge): "Wow, wow, [the] rhinoceros beetle [is] huge." ゴミムシか。 uses the noun ゴミムシ (carrion beetle) + question か: "[Is it a] carrion beetle, ka." The vlogger is questioning the beetle's species. いました。いました。 uses the polite past existential いました (was [here]) repeated: "[It] was [here]. [It] was [here]." The repetition marks the excitement of spotting the beetle. まあ、どう? uses the filler まあ (well) + interrogative どう (how): "Well, how [is it]?" めっちゃ飛ぶ。 uses the Kansai adverb + verb: "[It] flies super [well]." お、早い。 uses the surprise marker お (oh) + i-adjective 早い (fast): "Oh, fast." めっちゃ跳ねるやん。 uses the Kansai adverb + verb 跳ねる (jumps) + Kansai tag やん (yan = じゃん): "[It] jumps super, yan." The Kansai やん replaces standard じゃん, a tag particle seeking agreement or expressing surprise. どっか行っちゃった。 uses the colloquial adverb どっか (somewhere = どこか contracted) + past completion 行っちゃった (went = 行く + てしまう past): "[It] went somewhere." The completion ちゃった marks the beetle's departure as accidental or regrettable. めっちゃ大きい。でかない。 repeats the size admiration + Kansai tag question. 低のサイズや。 uses the ASR-unclear 低 (tei, low) + genitive の + noun サイズ (size) + Kansai copula や: "[It's a] low size, ya." The ASR rendered this as 低のサイズ, which likely means "small size" or "low-grade size" in the context of beetle collecting. ちょっとこっち向いて。 uses the adverb ちょっと (a bit) + demonstrative こっち (this way) + imperative 向いて (turn = 向く te-form imperative): "Turn this way a bit." お目目が可愛い。 uses the reduplicated/affectionate noun お目目 (o-meme, eyes [child language/affectionate]) + subject が + i-adjective 可愛い (cute): "[Its] eyes [are] cute." The reduplication 目目 with honorific prefix お creates an affectionate, childlike tone when describing the beetle's eyes.

The pattern [verb] + ところ + やった describes a near-miss situation: "[it] was the moment/plac where [X] would have happened."

Big." 何かに食べられた後ある。 uses the indefinite 何かに (by something) + passive past 食べられた (was eaten) + noun 後 (evidence/trace) + existential ある (there is): "[There's] evidence of [something] having been eaten by something." 野生の赤実や。 uses the noun phrase 野生の赤実 (wild red berries) + Kansai copula や: "[It's] wild red berries, ya." こんな山奥でめっちゃ赤実の匂いする。 uses the demonstrative phrase こんな山奥で (in such deep mountains) + Kansai adverb + noun phrase + verb する (smells): "In such deep mountains, [there's] a super smell of red berries." こんな山奥で1本だけ赤実生えてる。 uses the same locative phrase + number+counter 1本だけ (only one [plant]) + progressive 生えてる (is growing): "In such deep mountains, [there's] only one red berry [bush] growing." お、なんかいそうやけどな。 uses the realization marker + adverb なんか + conjectural いそう (seems [there is]) + Kansai やけどな (ya kedo na = だけどな): "Oh, [it] seems like something [is there], ya kedo na." そや、そや。昨日ここ切り倒されてたとこだ。 uses the Kansai affirmation そや (that's right = そうだ) repeated + temporal 昨日 (yesterday) + locative ここ (here) + passive past progressive 切り倒されてた (was being cut down = 切り倒す passive + てい) + noun とこ (place) + copula だ: "That's right, that's right. [This is] the place where [trees] were cut down here yesterday." なんか穴開いてるし。 uses the adverb なんか + noun 穴 (holes) + progressive 開いてる (are open = 開く + ている) + listing particle し: "Somehow [there are] holes open too, shi." The particle し lists reasons or observations. めっちゃ坂なんよ。 uses the Kansai adverb + noun 坂 (slope) + Kansai explanatory なんよ (nan yo = なんだよ): "[It's] super steep, nan yo." なんだか可愛いキノコ。 uses the adverb なんだか (somehow) + i-adjective 可愛い (cute) + noun キノコ (mushroom): "Somehow cute mushrooms." これもかじられてる。 uses the demonstrative + particle も (also) + passive progressive かじられてる (has been nibbled = かじる passive + ている): "This too [has been] nibbled." あ、水流れてる。 uses the realization marker + noun 水 + progressive 流てる: "Ah, water [is] flowing." 昨日ここで引き返した。 uses the temporal + locative + past 引き返した (turned back): "[I] turned back here yesterday." もう取るもんからカメラ取っていくわ。 uses the adverb もう (already) + noun phrase 取るもん (things [I'm] taking) + ablative から (from) + noun カメラ (camera) + directional 取っていく (will go take = 取る + ていく) + Kansai assertive わ: "[I'll] take the camera from the things [I'm] taking already, wa." 全部カメラ取る。 uses the universal 全部 (all) + noun カメラ + verb 取る (take): "[I'll] take all [with] the camera." ほ、2匹入った。 uses the interjection ほ (ho) + number+counter 2匹 (two animals) + past 入った (went in): "Ho, two [went] in." 誰も入ってこうへんから木が倒れたまんまだ。 uses the universal 誰も (no one) + Kansai negative 入ってこうへん (doesn't come in = 入ってくる + こうへん Kansai negative) + causal から (because) + noun phrase 木が倒れたまんま (tree fallen as-is = 木 + 倒れる + まんま) + copula だ: "Since no one comes in, the tree remains fallen as is." The Kansai こうへん replaces standard こない, and まんま replaces standard まま. 取れてますよ。 uses the progressive polite potential 取れてます (am able to get = 取れる + ています): "[I'm] getting [it], yo." え、なんだこれは? uses the surprise marker + interrogative phrase なんだこれは (what is this): "Huh, what is this?" セミ。昨日のカブトムシまだここにいてる。 uses the noun セミ (cicada) + temporal phrase 昨日のカブトムシ (yesterday's rhinoceros beetle) + adverb まだ (still) + locative ここに (here) + Kansai progressive いてる (is [here]): "[A] cicada. Yesterday's rhinoceros beetle [is] still here." 冷たい。あ、飛んでる。 uses the i-adjective 冷たい (cold) + realization + progressive 飛んでる (is flying): "Cold. Ah, [it's] flying." うん。これ何やったっけ? uses the affirmation うん (yeah) + demonstrative これ (this) + Kansai recall question 何やったっけ (what was it = 何だったっけ): "Yeah. What was this, kke?" 銭形やったっけ? uses the noun 銭形 (zenigata, a species name or pattern) + Kansai recall question やったっけ (was it = だったっけ): "[Was it] zenigata, kke?" 綺麗。びっくり。なるほど。 uses the i-adjective 綺麗 (beautiful) + noun びっくり (surprise) + set phrase なるほど (I see): "Beautiful. Surprising. [I] see." お、道消えてるんやけど。 uses the realization marker + noun 道 (road) + progressive 消えてる (is disappearing) + explanatory んやけど (n ya kedo = んだけど Kansai): "Oh, the road [is] disappearing, n ya kedo." もう道消えてる。もう引き返すか。 uses the adverb もう + same phrase + adverb もう + volitional question 引き返すか (shall [I] turn back): "Already the road [is] disappearing. [Shall I] turn back already, ka." 足元ガタガタ。 uses the noun 足元 (footing) + onomatopoeia ガタガタ (unstable; rickety): "[My] footing [is] unstable." すごい。なんかこの木すごいやん。 uses the i-adjective + adverb なんか + demonstrative phrase + i-adjective + Kansai tag やん: "Amazing. Somehow this tree [is] amazing, yan." 回して取れた。 uses the te-form 回して (twisting) + past potential 取れた (could get = 取れる past): "[I] got [it] by twisting."

05 Standard Vlog Outro

ご視聴ありがとうございました。チャンネル登録、高評価、コメントしてもらえると嬉しいです。InstagramとXのフォローもよろしくお願いします。メンバーシップもたまに更新しています。是非チェックしてください。それではまた次回の動画でお会いしましょう。
Goshichou arigatou gozaimashita. Channeru touroku, kouhyouka, komento shite moraeru to ureshii desu. Instagram to X no foroo mo yoroshiku onegai shimasu. Menbaashippu mo tama ni koushin shite imasu. Zehi chekku shite kudasai. Sore dewa mata jikai no douga de oai shimashou.
"Thank you for watching. [I] would be happy if [you] subscribe to the channel, give a high rating, and comment. Please also follow on Instagram and X. [I] occasionally update the membership [content] too. Please be sure to check [it]. Well then, let's meet again in the next video."

Grammar: This outro follows the standardized Japanese YouTube closing formula analyzed in previous breakdowns. ご視聴ありがとうございました uses the honorific ご + 視聴 + past polite ありがとうございました, marking gratitude for a completed action. チャンネル登録、高評価、コメントしてもらえると嬉しいです uses the noun list + てもらえると嬉しいです pattern: "[I] would be happy if [you] do [these things] for me." The potential もらえる frames viewer engagement as a favor to the creator. InstagramとXのフォローもよろしくお願いします uses も (also) + よろしくお願いします (please do so) for social media cross-promotion. メンバーシップもたまに更新しています uses the progressive polite 更新しています (am updating) with たまに (occasionally) to promote membership content without overpromising frequency. 是非チェックしてください uses the strong adverb 是非 (by all means) + imperative polite チェックしてください (please check) for emphatic promotion. それではまた次回の動画でお会いしましょう uses それでは (well then) + また (again) + locative 次回の動画で (in the next video) + honorific volitional お会いしましょう (let's meet): "Well then, let's meet again in the next video." The honorific お + 会いする elevates the audience, and the volitional creates a warm, forward-looking closing. This standardized outro contrasts with the casual, dialect-rich body of the video, demonstrating the code-switching between formal audience address and intimate rural narration.

06 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Dialect Core Particles and Copulas
や, やん, やった, やろう, やろか, やから, やけど, わ, な, へん, まへん, おる/おった/おられへん/いてまへん, そや, なんよ, やんと, かもや, ちゃん, やったっけ. The full Kansai grammatical system: copula や, tag やん, past やった, conjectural やろう/やろか, causal やから, concessive やけど, assertive わ, exclamatory な, negative へん, polite negative まへん, animate existential おる, affirmation そや, explanatory なんよ, and recall question やったっけ.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Regret)
飛んじゃった, 踏んじゃう, 行っちゃった. "Flew away;" "would step [on it];" "went somewhere." The grammar of accidental or regrettable completed actions in creature encounters.
〜のに (Concessive)
帰ろうと思ったのに. "Even though [I] thought [I'd] go home." The grammar of frustrated contrast between intention and continued discovery.
〜たら (Conditional and Desire)
見つけられたらなって思ってます. "[I'm] thinking [I'd] like to find [them]." The grammar of wishes and hopes using the passive potential conditional + な + 思う.
〜っぽい / 〜みたい (Similative)
茶色っぽい, 赤っぽい, おもちゃみたい, トンネルみたい. "Brownish;" "reddish;" "like a toy;" "like a tunnel." The grammar of approximation and visual comparison in nature observation.
Directional and Resultative Progressives
なってる, 食べられてる, かじられてる, 生えてる, 流れてる, 歩いてる, 消えてる, 飛んでる. "Has become;" "is being eaten;" "has been nibbled;" "is growing;" "is flowing;" "is walking;" "is disappearing;" "is flying." The grammar of ongoing states and processes in the forest environment.
Forest Exploration and Nature Vocabulary
林道, イノシシ, カブトムシ, ゴムシ, キノコ, 毒々しい, カエル, 赤ガエル, セミ, 樹液, 野生, 赤実, 山奥, 切り倒す, 坂, 穴, まんま, 足元. The specialized lexicon of Japanese forest road exploration: forest road, wild boar, rhinoceros beetle, carrion beetle, mushroom, vivid/lurid, frog, red frog, cicada, tree sap, wild, red berries, deep mountains, cut down, slope, hole, as-is, footing.
ASR Errors and Phonetic Reconstruction
安で/穴で, カムシ/カメ, 飛車安で/飛んじゃった, 低/小, パルプ/パルプ?, 赤実/赤字, 銭ごケ/銭形, ガッガ/ガタガタ, 回し取/回して取れた, まちゃん/ご視聴. The grammar of machine-transcribed rural Kansai speech where homophones, dialect phonology, and acoustic degradation create systematic lexical mismatches.
