07 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

〜ていく / 〜てくる (Directional Progressive)
穴を開けていきます, 埋めていきます, 置きに行きます, 置いていきます, 見えてくる. "[I'll] go drill holes;" "[I'll] go bury;" "[I'll] go put;" "starting to look." The grammar of spatially extended and gradually emerging processes in rural DIY work.
〜ように (Purpose and Result)
菌が早く回るように, 飛ばされないようにしたら. "So the fungus spreads quickly;" "if [I] make [it] so [they] won't be blown away." The grammar of purpose clauses and resultative states in agricultural work.
〜ておく (Preparatory Action)
湿らしておきました. "[I] moistened [them] in advance." The grammar of preparatory actions, essential for describing the curing and care of inoculated logs.
〜ていたら (Past Progressive Conditional)
育てていたら散乱して羽化しました. "When [I] was raising [them], [they] scattered and emerged." The grammar of temporal "when" from ongoing past actions, marking the origin story of the beetle colony.
Kansai Dialect Core
わ, や, やん, やった, あかん, かもしれへん, ちっちゃい, めっちゃ, ちゃう, へん, もん. The negative へん, the copula や, the tag やん, the past やった, the prohibitive あかん, the conjectural かもしれへん, the colloquial ちっちゃい, the adverb めっちゃ, the variant ちゃう, and the complaining もん form the emotional texture of Kansai rural narration.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Regret)
使い切って, 入れちゃった. "[I] used up;" "[I] put [them] all in [regrettably]." The grammar of completed actions, often with a sense of finality or minor regret.
〜方がいい (Comparative Recommendation)
原木を地面から浮かせた方がいい. "[It] would be better to raise the logs off the ground." The grammar of recommendations and best practices in rural cultivation.
