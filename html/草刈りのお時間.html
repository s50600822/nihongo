01 Introduction — New Grass Trimmer and Field Work

こんにちは、くまです。今日は天気がいいので畑作業をしていきます。最近我が家にやってきた草刈り機を使って、今日は草を刈っていきます。着いたら早速草を刈っていきます。こちらの草刈り機に実はすごいところがあって、草が絡まてもボタン1つで解消してくれます。今までの草刈り機だったらその都度エンジンを止めて草を取ったりと大変でしたが、この機能でとても楽になりました。この調子でどんどん刈っていきます。
Konnichiwa, Kuma desu. Kyou wa tenki ga ii node hatake sagyou wo shite ikimasu. Saikin wagaya ni yatte kita kusakari-ki wo tsukatte, kyou wa kusa wo katte ikimasu. Grammar: こんにちは、くまです。 is the standard vlogger opening with name + polite copula. 今日は天気がいいので畑作業をしていきます。 uses the topic 今日は (today [topic]) + subject 天気が (weather [subject]) + i-adjective いい (good) + causal ので (because) + object 畑作業を (field work [object]) + directional progressive していきます (will go do): "Today the weather is good so [I'll] go do field work." The causal ので marks good weather as the reason for outdoor work. 最近我が家にやってきた草刈り機を使って、今日は草を刈っていきます。 uses the temporal 最近 (recently) + locative 我が家に (to our home) + directional perfective やってきた (came = やってくる + た) modifying 草刈り機を (grass trimmer [object]) + instrumental te-form 使って (using) + topic 今日は (today [topic]) + object 草を (grass [object]) + directional progressive 刈っていきます (will go cut): "Using the grass trimmer that recently came to our home, today [I'll] go cut grass." The ASR omitted the 機 from 草刈り機. 着いたら早速草を刈っていきます。 uses the conditional 着いたら (once [I] arrive = 着く + いたら) + adverb 早速 (promptly) + object 草を + directional progressive 刈っていきます: "Once [I] arrive, [I'll] promptly go cut grass." The ASR error 買っていきます for 刈っていきます is a classic homophone confusion where 買う (buy) replaces 刈る (cut) due to identical pronunciation in rapid speech. こちらの草刈り機に実はすごいところがあって uses the polite demonstrative こちらの (this) + topic 草刈り機に (grass trimmer [topic]) + adverb 実は (actually) + i-adjective すごい (amazing) + subject ところが (feature [subject]) + existential あって (there is = ある + て): "This grass trimmer actually has an amazing feature." 草が絡まってもボタン1つで解消してくれます。 uses the subject 草が (grass [subject]) + conditional 絡まっても (even if tangled = 絡まる + ても) + instrumental phrase ボタン1つで (with one button) + verb 解消してくれます (resolves for me = 解消する + て + くれる): "Even if grass gets tangled, one button resolves it for me." The conditional ても marks the tangling as a hypothetical obstacle, and the benefactive くれる frames the machine as doing the favor for the user. 今までの草刈り機だったらその都度エンジンを止めて草を取ったりと大変でしたが uses the counterfactual conditional だったら (if [it] was) + temporal その都度 (each time) + object エンジンを (engine [object]) + te-form 止めて (stopping) + object 草を (grass [object]) + verb pattern 取ったりと (removing etc. = 取る + たり + と) + na-adjective 大変でした (was tough) + contrastive が (but): "With previous grass trimmers, [I] had to stop the engine each time and remove grass, which was tough, but..." The たりと pattern lists representative actions in a sequence. この機能でとても楽になりました。 uses the instrumental この機能で (with this function) + adverb とても (very) + na-adjective 楽に (easily) + verb なりました (became): "With this function [it] has become very easy." この調子でどんどん刈っていきます。 uses the instrumental この調子で (in this manner = この + 調子 + で) + adverb どんどん (steadily) + directional progressive 刈っていきます (will go cut): "In this manner [I'll] keep cutting more and more."

02 Break, Weather, and Resuming the Work

Tsugi wa are-houdai no hatake wo kirei ni shite ikimasu. Kono toki tebukuro ga nai koto ni kizukimashita. E, dokka? Katte mo katte mo hette iru ki ga shimasen. Ima ni mo nagedashitai desu ga, tsuzukete ikimasu.
"Here [I'll] take a short break. On this day the weather was good and a pleasant wind was blowing. It's about time to resume grass cutting. If [I] cut half more, the grass cutting will be finished. Even though [I] cut and cut, [I] don't feel [it's] decreasing. [I] want to give up any moment now, but [I'll] keep continuing."

Grammar: ここでちょっと休憩です。 uses the locative ここで (here) + adverb ちょっと (short) + noun 休憩です (is a break): "Here [I'll] take a short break." この日は天気も良く心地よい風が吹いていました。 uses the topic この日は (on this day [topic]) + subject 天気も (weather [also/subject]) + adverbial i-adjective 良く (well) + i-adjective 心地よい (pleasant) + subject 風が (wind [subject]) + progressive polite past 吹いていました (was blowing): "On this day the weather was good and a pleasant wind was blowing." The adverbial form 良く modifies the following adjective phrase, and も adds emphasis to the weather being good as well. そろそろ草刈り再開です。 uses the adverb そろそろ (about time) + noun 草刈り再開です (is grass-cutting resumption): "It's about time to resume grass cutting." あと半分刈ったら草刈りは終わりです。 uses the conditional 刈ったら (if [I] cut) + topic 草刈りは (grass cutting [topic]) + noun 終わりです (is the end): "If [I] cut half more, the grass cutting will be finished." The remaining amount あと半分 (half more) marks the progress point. 頑張っていきます。 uses the te-form 頑張って (doing one's best) + directional いきます (will go): "[I'll] do [my] best and keep going." 次は荒れ放題の畑を綺麗にしていきます。 uses the topic 次は (next [topic]) + object 荒れ放題の畑を (completely overgrown field [object]) + adverbial 綺麗に (cleanly) + directional progressive していきます (will go make): "Next [I'll] go clean up the field that has been left completely overgrown." The compound 荒れ放題 combines the stem of 荒れる (run wild) with 放題 (as one pleases; without restraint) to mean "completely overgrown." この時手袋がないことに気づきました。 uses the temporal この時 (at this time) + subject 手袋が (gloves [subject]) + negative existential ない (not exist) + nominalizer ことに (to the fact that) + verb 気づきました (noticed): "At this point [I] noticed [I] don't have gloves." The pattern [plain form] + ことに気づく means "to notice that [plain form]." え、どっか？ uses the surprise marker え (huh?) + colloquial interrogative どっか (where = どこか): "Huh, where?" 刈っても刈っても減っている気がしません。 uses the concessive repetition 刈っても刈っても (even if [I] cut and cut = 刈る + ても repeated) + progressive 減っている (is decreasing) + nominalized phrase 気がしません (don't feel = 気がする negative): "Even though [I] cut and cut, [I] don't feel [it's] decreasing." The ASR error 勝っても for 刈っても is a phonetic near-miss where かって (katte) could be either 買って or 刈って, and the ASR chose the wrong homophone. The pattern [te-form] + も repeated marks persistent effort without result. 今にも投げ出したいですが、続けていきます。 uses the temporal phrase 今にも (any moment now) + desiderative 投げ出したい (want to give up = 投げ出す + たい) + polite ですが (but) + directional progressive 続けていきます (will continue): "[I] want to give up any moment now, but [I'll] keep continuing." The desiderative たい expresses the vlogger's urge to quit, and the contrastive が marks her perseverance despite the urge.

The passive marks the vlogger as the recipient of John's attention. お前は散歩に行きたくて荒ぶるジョンに頭突きをして散歩に連れていけと催促してきます。 uses the pronoun お前は (you [topic]) + desiderative phrase 散歩に行きたくて (wanting to go for a walk = 行きたい + くて) + attributive 荒ぶる (rampaging) modifying ジョンに (John [to]) + object 頭突きを (headbutt [object]) + te-form して (doing) + quotative 散歩に連れていけと ("take me for a walk" = imperative of 連れて行く + と) + noun 催促 (urging) + directional してきます (comes doing = する + て + くる): "You, wanting to go for a walk, come urging [me] to take [you] for a walk by headbutting [me], [you] rampaging John." The pattern [verb] + まで marks the endpoint of the waiting period.

06 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

〜ていく / 〜てくる (Directional Progressive and Perfective)
畑作業をしていきます, 草を刈っていきます, 抜いていきます, 続けていきます, 頑張っていきます, 綺麗にしていきます. "[I'll] go do field work;" "[I'll] go cut grass;" "[I'll] go pull [them] out;" "[I'll] keep continuing;" "[I'll] do [my] best and keep going;" "[I'll] go clean up." The grammar of forward-moving work processes in rural labor narration.
Kansai Dialect Core — や, やん, んや, あかん, けん
生えてたんや (were growing = のだ), 気づけんかった (didn't notice = 気づけなかった), あかんで (no good = 駄目で), あかん (no good). The Kansai explanatory んや, negative けん, and prohibitive あかん form the emotional texture of rural Kansai narration.
〜ても (Concessive Conditional)
刈っても刈っても減っている気がしません (even if [I] cut and cut, [I] don't feel [it's] decreasing), 草が絡まってもボタン1つで解消してくれます (even if grass gets tangled, one button resolves it). The grammar of persistent effort without result and automatic problem resolution.
〜たい (Desiderative) and 〜たがる (Third-Person Desire)
散歩に行きたくて (wanting to go for a walk), 投げ出したい (want to give up), 出てきて欲しい (want [them] to come out). The grammar of expressing one's own desires and attributing desires to animals.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Result)
持ってきちゃった (brought along unnecessarily), 隠れちゃった (hid completely). The grammar of completed actions with unintended or notable consequences.
〜みたい, 〜よう, 〜そう (Appearance and Inference)
興味がないみたいです (doesn't seem interested), 不満なようです (seems dissatisfied), まだ食べれそうな (still seems edible), 高そうなバッグ (expensive-looking bag). The grammar of visual and contextual inference about states and qualities.
〜てくれる / 〜てもらう (Benefactive)
解消してくれます (resolves it for me), 教えていただける (could tell me). The grammar of actions done as a favor for the speaker or by the speaker for others.
〜たりと, 〜たり (Representative Action Listing)
草を取ったりと大変でした (removing grass etc. was tough), 消えちゃったり (disappearing, among other things). The grammar of listing representative actions in a sequence.
Onomatopoeia and Sensory Vocabulary
チクチク (prickling), ボコボコ (bumpy), バクバク (puffing), コロンコロン (round pellets), ちらほら (here and there), どんどん (steadily). The grammar of Japanese sound-symbolic vocabulary describing physical sensations, terrain, and pace of work.
ASR Errors and Phonetic Reconstruction
買っていきます→刈っていきます, 草刈り→草刈り機, ついたら→着いたら, 勝っても→刈っても, アラブル→荒ぶる, 頭つき→頭突き, 最速→催促, 背た→背丈, かんどん→簡単に, じゃがいいも/じがいも→じゃがいも, あじさん→紫蘇, ナスび→ナス, 背筋スメ→セスジスズメ, カキリ→カット, 生えてたんや→生えてたんや, 気づけんかった→気づけなかった, ジ→じゃあ. The grammar of machine-transcribed rural Kansai speech where homophones, dialect phonology, and acoustic degradation create systematic lexical mismatches.
Gardening and Rural Life Vocabulary
草刈り機 (grass trimmer), 畑作業 (field work), 長靴 (rubber boots), 雑草 (weeds), じゃがいも (potato), 紫蘇 (shiso), ナス (eggplant), 青じそ (green shiso), セスジスズメ (hawkmoth), 幼虫 (caterpillar/larva), 収穫 (harvest), 雑草ソファー (weed sofa). The specialized lexicon of Japanese rural gardening and foraging.
Animal Interaction and Command Grammar
荒ぶるジョン (rampaging John), 頭突きをして (headbutting), 催促してきます (comes urging), ダメ (no), あかん (no good), 待ってね (wait), バイバイ (bye-bye), よし (good), よいしょ (heave-ho). The grammar of imperatives, negative commands, and affectionate scolding directed at animals.
