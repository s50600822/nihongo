Essay Grammar Patterns

These patterns appear frequently in formal Japanese essays and literary prose. Each entry shows the pattern, romaji, function, and an example.

Nominalization & Abstraction

〜こと
~koto

Function: Nominalizes a verb clause into a noun phrase. Common in reflective writing.

Example: 生きたこと (ikita koto) — "the fact of having lived"

〜の
~mono

Function: Nominalizer with an emotional or experiential nuance. "The thing that..."

Example: 忘れられないもの (wasurerarenai mono) — "something unforgettable"

〜のである / 〜んだ
~no de aru / ~n da

Function: Explanatory/emphatic copula. Gives essays their characteristic authoritative tone.

Example: それが真実のである (sore ga shinjitsu no de aru) — "That is the truth."

〜という
~to iu

Function: Quotative / definitional marker. "The so-called..." / "What is called..."

Example: 20世紀という時代 (nijisseiki to iu jidai) — "the era called the 20th century"

Passive & Humble Forms

〜れる / 〜られる (passive)
~reru / ~rareru

Function: Passive voice. Common in historical narrative to focus on events rather than agents.

Example: 戦争に巻き込まれる (sensō ni makikomareru) — "to be caught up in war"

〜させられる (causative-passive)
~saserareru

Function: Forced action. "Was made to..." Conveys powerlessness.

Example: 苦しみを味わさせられる (kurushimi wo ajiwasaserareru) — "was made to taste suffering"

お〜になる / 〜なさる (honorific)
o~ni naru / ~nasaru

Function: Honorific verbs when describing actions of respected figures.

Example: 天皇がおっしゃった (tennō ga ossharuta) — "the Emperor said"

Conjunctions & Transitions

〜一方(で)
~ippō (de)

Function: "On the one hand..." / "while..." Shows contrast between two aspects.

Example: 繁栄した一方で、問題も残った。(han'ei shita ippō de, mondai mo nokotta.)

〜反面
~hanmen

Function: "On the contrary / on the other hand." Stronger contrast than 一方.

Example: 技術は進歩した反面、環境は破壊された。(gijutsu wa shinpo shita hanmen, kankyō wa hakai sareta.)

つまり
tsumari

Function: "In other words, that is to say." Clarifies or rephrases.

すなわち
sunawachi

Function: Formal "in other words, namely." More literary than つまり.

しかしながら
shikashi nagara

Function: Formal "however, nevertheless." Common in essays and speeches.

なぜら(〜からだ)
nazenara (~kara da)

Function: "The reason is that..." / "because." Gives justification.

Modality & Hedging

〜ように思う
~yō ni omou

Function: "I think that..." Softens a statement. Common in personal essays.

〜のではないか
~no de wa nai ka

Function: "Is it not the case that...?" / "I wonder if..." Rhetorical questioning.

〜べきだ
~beki da

Function: "Should / ought to." Expresses obligation or recommendation.

Example: 歴史から学ぶべきだ (rekishi kara manabu beki da) — "We should learn from history."

〜に違いない
~ni chigai nai

Function: "Must be / no doubt." Strong inference.

Example: 彼は正しいに違いない (kare wa tadashii ni chigai nai) — "He must be right."

Key Phrases for This Book

These phrases are likely to recur or appear in introductions and transitions across the essays.

Opening & Framing

20世紀を振り返ると
Nijisseiki wo furikaeru to
"Looking back at the 20th century..." — Common essay opening.
私にとって20世紀とは
Watashi ni totte nijisseiki to wa
"For me, the 20th century is..." — Personal framing device.
〜の記憶は今も鮮明だ
~no kioku wa ima mo senmei da
"The memory of ~ is still vivid." — Emotional recollection.
生きた証しとして
Ikita akashi to shite
"As testimony of having lived." — Central theme of the book.
体験にもとづいて
Taiken ni motozuite
"Based on (my) experience." — Signals personal narrative.

Evaluation & Reflection

〜ということは忘れてはならない
~to iu koto wa wasurete wa naranai
"We must not forget that..." — Strong admonition.
〜に尽きる
~ni kiru
"Nothing but ~ / It all comes down to ~" — "感謝に尽きる" (nothing but gratitude).
〜に値する
~ni atai suru
"To be worthy of ~ / deserve ~" — Formal evaluation.
〜を前提として
~wo zentei to shite
"On the premise of ~ / assuming ~" — Logical framing.
〜ゆえに
~yue ni
"Because of ~ / therefore." Literary/archaic causal connector.
〜とはいえ
~to wa ie
"Having said that / although." Concessive transition.

Time & Change

時代の流れとともに
Jidai no nagare to tomo ni
"Along with the flow of time / as times change."
〜を経て
~wo hete
"Through ~ / via ~" — "戦争を経て" (through the war).
〜に至るまで
~ni itaru made
"Up until ~ / all the way to ~" — Temporal scope.
〜を境に
~wo sakai ni
"With ~ as a turning point." — "敗戦を境に" (with defeat as the turning point).
〜に至る
~ni itaru
"To reach / lead to ~" — Causal or temporal culmination.

Emotion & Subjectivity

〜と感じる
~to kanjiru
"I feel that ~" — Subjective marker.
〜を胸に
~wo mune ni
"With ~ in one's heart." Poetic emotional framing.
〜に耐える
~ni taeru
"To endure ~ / withstand ~" — "苦しみに耐える" (endure suffering).
〜に打ち勝つ
~ni kachiu-katsu
"To overcome ~" — "困難に打ち勝つ" (overcome difficulties).
〜に満ちた
~ni michita
"Full of ~ / filled with ~" — "希望に満ちた" (full of hope).
