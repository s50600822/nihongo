The manga famously places the furigana 進化 (shinka, evolution) over 悪意 (akui, malice), creating the chilling implication: humanity's malice is its evolution.

05 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

〜てやる
"To do something for someone" (from a superior position) — carries contemptuous or paternalistic tone.
〜とは言えぬまでも
"Although one cannot call it ~" — concessive clause expressing qualified agreement.
〜ておく
"To do something in advance / as preparation" — adds declarative or preparatory force.
〜ちゃいねぇ / 〜じゃねェ
Rough masculine contractions of 〜ていない / ではない — Netero's signature rough speech.
〜のだ / 〜んだ
Explanatory/emphatic copula — asserts a fact with conviction or explanation.
〜べき
"Ought to; deserving of" — expresses obligation, worthiness, or moral desirability.
〜まい
Classical negative conjectural — "surely does not / surely will not."
〜ならば
Formal conditional — "if that is the case / if so."
〜てやらんでもない
Double negative — "it is not that I would not ~" i.e., "I might possibly ~."
