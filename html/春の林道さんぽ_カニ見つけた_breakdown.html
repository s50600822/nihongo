07 Key Grammar Patterns Summary

Kansai Dialect Core — や, やん, やな, やけど, やから, やった, へん, おる, なか, わ
山びこや (is), 風が強いやな (is, na), 大変やな (is, na), おった (was there), 見えへん (can't see), 喋られん (can't speak), わからん (don't know), 帰れない (can't go home), 食べようかな (shall we eat?), いてんの (is it there?), いてんや (is there, ya), やないか (isn't it?), いたんちゃん (there were, chan), んちゃうかな (wasn't it?). The Kansai copula や, assertive やん, explanatory やな, negative へん, existential おる, and past やった form the emotional backbone of rural Kansai narration.
〜ている / 〜てん Progressive and Existential Contractions
水流れてる (water is flowing), 魚いてんじゃん (there are fish!), めっちゃいてるわ (there are lots), ちっちゃいけどいっぱいいてんね (tiny but lots are there), なんかいてんかな (wonder if something's there), 亀浮いてる (turtle is floating), 桜が咲いてる (cherry blossoms are blooming), キノコ生えてる (mushrooms are growing), 泳いでる (swimming). The grammar of Kansai-contracted progressives where ている becomes てる or てん, and いる becomes いてん.
〜てしまう / 〜ちゃった (Completion and Regret)
持ってきちゃった (brought along unnecessarily), 隠れちゃった (hid completely), 消えちゃったり (disappearing), 行っちゃった (went away). The grammar of completed actions with a sense of regret, disappearance, or unintended consequence.
〜てくる / 〜ていく (Directional Perfective and Progressive)
雨降ってきてない (hasn't started raining), 出てきて欲しい (want [them] to come out), 集まってきた (gathered), 食べていきます (will go eat). The grammar of actions approaching or moving away from the speaker.
〜そう (Appearance Inference)
高そうなバッグ (expensive-looking bag), 気持ち良さそうに浮いてる (floating looking comfortable), 美味しそうなキノコ (delicious-looking mushroom), ジョン喜びそう (John would probably be happy), 美味しそうやね (looks delicious, ya ne). The grammar of visual and contextual inference.
〜っぽい, 〜みたい, 〜ように (Resemblance and Similative)
赤耳ガメっぽい (looks like a red-eared slider), トンネルみたいになってんで (has become like a tunnel), 気持ち良さそうに (comfortably). The grammar of resemblance, similarity, and manner.
